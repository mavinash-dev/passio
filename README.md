# Passio *(working name — final name TBD)*

> Where creator passion meets buyer discovery.

---

## What It Is

Passio is a discovery platform for products made by Indian creators and influencers.

Creators list their own brands and products. Buyers discover and shop from creators they already trust — or find new ones. Nobody handles shipping, payments, or logistics. Pure visibility, pure discovery.

**Positioning:** Nykaa — but for all categories, all creator brands, not just established labels.

## The Problem

Indian creators are building real products — skincare, food, fitness, fashion, lifestyle. But their reach is capped at their existing followers. No platform exists to help buyers discover creator-owned brands. No Amazon for passion-built products. No Myntra for creator labels.

## Why Now

Creator-led D2C is exploding in India (Mamaearth, Wow Skin Science, Mcaffeine all started this way). The next wave is micro and mid-tier creators with their own products and zero discoverability outside their audience.
