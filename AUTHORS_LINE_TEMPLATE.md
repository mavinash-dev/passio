# Author’s Line Template

Add this section at the end of every brand story or profile to share your perspective as the curator/editor.

---

**Author’s Line Example:**

> "What inspired me most about [Brand Name] is their relentless focus on [unique trait, value, or journey]. In a space crowded with trends, their commitment to [core value or differentiator] stands out. — [Your Name], Passio"

---

**How to Use:**
- Summarize what you found most compelling about the brand or founder.
- Highlight a value, trait, or story that sets them apart.
- Sign off with your name or "Team Passio" for a personal touch.
