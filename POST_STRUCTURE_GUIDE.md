# Passio Brand Story/Post Structure Guide

## 1. Brand Introduction
- Brand name, founder(s), and what they do
- Quick context: category, origin, and what makes them interesting

## 2. The Journey
- How and why the brand started
- Key challenges, turning points, or moments of growth

## 3. The Spirit (What Drives Them)
- The values, dreams, or purpose behind the brand
- What keeps them going, what they want to change or achieve

## 4. The Product(s)
- What they offer, what’s unique about their products
- Any signature item or story behind a product

## 5. Author’s Line
- Your personal takeaway or what inspired you about this brand (see AUTHORS_LINE_TEMPLATE.md)

---

**Tips:**
- Keep it authentic and story-driven, not just a list of facts.
- Use quotes from the founder if possible.
- End with a call to action or invitation to discover more on Passio.
